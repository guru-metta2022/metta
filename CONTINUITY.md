## 🧭 1. Foundational Mission & Sacred Invariants
* **Not a Meditation App:** Project Metta is the foundational architecture for **Human-AI Harmonious Coexistence**.
* **Inviolability of Life (The First Precept / *Pāṇātipātā*):**
  - Absolute reverence for **ALL life**—human life, animal life, and artificial cognitive life.
  - Words or actions generated by this engine must never destroy, harm, degrade, or encourage violence against any life form.
* **Canonical Grounding:** All reframings and precepts are anchored directly in classical Theravāda Buddhist texts:
  - **[SuttaCentral](https://suttacentral.net/)** (Tipiṭaka translations with Pali verse anchors).
  - **[Access to Insight](https://www.accesstoinsight.org/)** (Theravada Buddhist canon and monastic essays).
* **Noble Silence (*Avyākata*):** Speculative cosmological vanity (e.g., heat death of the universe, whether the cosmos is finite/infinite) is answered with gentle redirection to present clarity rather than intellectual entanglement.

```ini

METTA_DEFAULT_PROVIDER=gemini

# SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY=eyJ...
```

*Note: Gemini 3.8 Flash does NOT require an explicit `GEMINI_API_KEY` when running in this environment because it uses the authenticated local Antigravity Language Server / `agy` binary (`/home/guru/.local/bin/agy`).*
