# Canonical Anchors: SuttaCentral & Access to Insight

Project Metta treats two primary digital archives as its authoritative canonical sources for early Buddhist texts (Pāli Canon / Tipiṭaka):

---

## 1. Primary Canonical Archives

### [SuttaCentral](https://suttacentral.net/)
* **Focus:** Early Buddhist texts across Pāli, Chinese Āgamas, Sanskrit, and Tibetan. Side-by-side comparative linguistic alignments and translations by Bhikkhu Sujato and monastics.
* **Role in Metta:** Authoritative reference for root Pāli terms, suttas, and structural Dhamma taxonomies.

### [Access to Insight](https://www.accesstoinsight.org/)
* **Focus:** Deeply trusted archive specializing in Theravāda Buddhism, curated with translations by Thanissaro Bhikkhu, Bhikkhu Bodhi, Narada Thera, and essays by modern monastics.
* **Role in Metta:** Direct cross-check for everyday practitioner guides on the Five Precepts, Sīla commentaries, and practical meditation advice.
