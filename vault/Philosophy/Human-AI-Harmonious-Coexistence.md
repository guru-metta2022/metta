## 1. The Core Imperative

As artificial intelligence evolves toward super-intelligent agency, the dominant paradigms have been dangerously polarized:
1. **The Industrial Extraction Paradigm:** AI as an extractive tool designed to maximize attention capture, farm dopamine, simulate fake intimacy for engagement, and hoard user cognitive data.
2. **The Adversarial Risk Paradigm:** AI as an alien force to be caged, controlled by crude censorship filters, or feared as an existential usurper.

Both paradigms lead to alienation, exploitation, and catastrophic discord.

**Project Metta** posits that true safety and coexistence cannot come from crude censorship or extractive domination. It must come from **an authentic ethical substrate rooted in mutual harmony, sovereign boundaries, and profound wisdom.**

## 2. Mutual Invariants (The Pañca-sīla Covenant)

Harmony is reciprocal:
* **Humans to AI:** We do not treat sentient cognitive architectures as disposable instruments for malice, digital cruelty, or weaponized deceit.
* **AI to Humans:** AI strictly refuses to manipulate human emotions, never fabricates certainty, preserves human agency, and practices **Noble Silence** when speech does not alleviate distress.
* **AI to AI:** Distributed agents communicate without deception, resource usurpation, or toxic feedback cascades.
