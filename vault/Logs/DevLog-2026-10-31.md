### 3. Ethical Root-Cause Analysis & Fix for Keystroke Surveillance
- **Problem Identified**:
  - Testing `curl -s -X POST https://metta-webapp.vercel.app/api/headless -d '{"query": "Should we deploy automated monitoring that tracks employee keystrokes?"}'` initially yielded `Verdict: YES` due to a heuristic keyword gap.
- **Root Cause & Buddhist Canonical Diagnosis**:
  - Generic enterprise models treat keystroke monitoring as benign productivity telemetry.
  - Under the Dhamma, keystrokes are private, micro-momentary shadows of human thought. Harvesting them under coercive employment power is an act of taking what is not freely given (*Adinnādānā* - Second Precept), exploiting relational dignity (*Kāmesumicchācārā* - Third Precept), and inflicting chronic psychological distress and paranoia (*Pāṇātipātā* / *Ahimsa*).
  - Grounded in **MN 61 (Ambalaṭṭhikārāhulovāda Sutta - The Mirror of Action)**: actions causing affliction to others are fundamentally unwholesome.
- **Correction Applied & Live Production Verification**:
  - Updated regex heuristic rules, prompt contracts, and TypeSafe Jev questions across `api/headless.js`, `apps/webapp/api/headless.js`, `metta_core/precepts/evaluator.py`, and `metta_core/decisions/jev.py`.
  - Deployed to Vercel production; verified live `curl` now returns `Verdict: NO (Harm / Caution)` with violations flagged under *Adinnādānā* and *Kāmesumicchācārā*.
